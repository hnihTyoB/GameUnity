🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING:

Shadow không spawn?
  → Check prefabs assigned, zones active, Console errors

Shadow spawn vào tường?
  → Set Obstacle Layer, check wall layers, tăng check radius

Shadow spawn quá gần player?
  → Tăng Min Distance From Player

Performance drops?
  → Giảm Max Shadows, tăng Spawn Interval
